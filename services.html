What we do

Five service lines, one desk. Most clients start with a valuation and stay through the sale, the financing and the asset that replaces it.

Investment Sales

Sell-side representation from pricing through recording. We build the model, assemble the diligence room, run a structured marketing period and manage the buyer through escrow.

  • Broker opinion of value with full comparable set
  • Offering memorandum and financial model
  • Call-for-offers process and bid analysis

Leasing & Representation

Landlord representation to stabilise vacancy, and tenant representation for occupiers who need the right building at the right basis.

  • Market rent surveys and concession benchmarking
  • Lease structuring, TI negotiation and renewals
  • Site selection and occupancy cost modelling

Debt & Equity Placement

We take the financing to market alongside the sale so pricing holds. Agency, bank, life company, bridge and preferred equity.

  • Acquisition, refinance and construction debt
  • JV equity and preferred structures
  • Loan assumption and defeasance management

1031 Exchange Advisory

Forty-five days to identify, one hundred and eighty to close. We run replacement searches in parallel with the downleg so the clock never beats you.

  • Off-market replacement sourcing
  • Qualified intermediary coordination
  • Backup identification strategy

Valuation & Advisory

Broker opinions of value for estate planning, partnership buyouts, lender reporting and hold-versus-sell decisions.

  • Three-day turnaround BOVs
  • Hold / sell and refinance sensitivity analysis
  • Highest and best use studies

Asset Management

For out-of-state and absentee owners: vendor oversight, capex planning, compliance tracking and quarterly reporting against a written business plan.

  • Quarterly owner reporting packages
  • Soft-story and ADA compliance tracking
  • Capital expenditure planning and bidding
How An Assignment Runs

From first call to recording

A sell-side assignment typically takes ninety to a hundred and twenty days end to end.

01

Valuation

We tour the asset, audit the rent roll, normalise the T-12 and deliver a written BOV with the comparable set behind it. Days 1–3.

02

Preparation

Photography, offering memorandum, financial model and a complete diligence room assembled before a single buyer is contacted. Days 4–21.

03

Marketing

Targeted outreach to a matched buyer list, tours, then a published call for offers with a hard date. Days 22–50.

04

Close

Bid analysis, buyer qualification, escrow management and lender coordination through to recording. Days 51–110.
