Why AI Capital CRE

We underwrite before we market.

Every assignment starts with a full financial model — rent roll audit, T-12 normalisation, market rent survey and an exit sensitivity table. Buyers get a package they can act on, and sellers get pricing they can defend.

  • Institutional-grade underwritingFull Argus-equivalent models on every listing, not a one-page flyer.
  • A real buyer list, not a blast1,400 qualified principals tracked by product type, cheque size and 1031 timing.
  • Debt and equity in-houseWe place the financing alongside the sale, so deals close rather than retrade.
  • Principal-level attentionYou work with the broker who signed the listing, through to recording.
