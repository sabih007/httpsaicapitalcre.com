Our Story

Founded on the Crenshaw corridor.

AI Capital CRE was formed in Los Angeles to serve the owners that the national brokerages quote but rarely service: families holding three buildings, sponsors on their second syndication, operators with a 1031 clock running. These are assignments where a two-percentage-point pricing error is the difference between a good decade and a bad one, and where the broker who pitched the listing needs to be the broker who closes it.

We built the firm around one discipline: nothing goes to market before it is underwritten the way an institutional buyer will underwrite it. Rent roll audited line by line. T-12 normalised for non-recurring items. Market rents surveyed in person, not pulled from a subscription. An exit sensitivity table that shows what happens if cap rates move fifty basis points against you.

It is slower to prepare and far harder to retrade. Across 210 transactions we have averaged 98.4% of asking price and 47 days to close, and eighty-one percent of our business now comes from clients we have already represented or who were referred by one.

How We Work

Four commitments

These are the rules we will not break to win an assignment.

We price it honestly

We will not buy a listing with a number we cannot defend with comparables. If our valuation is below what you hoped, you will hear that in the first meeting.

Nothing goes out half-ready

No listing is released until the model, the OM and the diligence room are complete. A buyer who has to chase documents is a buyer who retrades.

You get the principal

The broker who signs your listing runs your file through to recording. No handoff to a junior after the agreement is executed.

We stay in our markets

If we cannot speak to the last ten comparable trades on the street, we refer the assignment out. Coverage without knowledge is just advertising.
